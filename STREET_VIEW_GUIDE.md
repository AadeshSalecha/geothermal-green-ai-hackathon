# 🌍 Street View AR Feature Guide

Your geothermal globe now includes an immersive Street View-like experience for ground-level exploration!

## 🎯 **How to Use Street View**

### **Entering Street View:**
1. **Right-click** anywhere on the globe
2. Camera automatically flies to ground level (10m above surface)
3. A red pin marks your location
4. You're now in first-person exploration mode!

### **Navigation in Street View:**
- **Mouse**: Look around (360° view)
- **WASD Keys**: Move around like a first-person game
  - `W` - Move forward
  - `S` - Move backward  
  - `A` - Move left
  - `D` - Move right
- **Mouse Wheel**: Zoom in/out
- **Drag**: Pan the view

### **Exiting Street View:**
- Click the **green eye button** in the top-right controls
- Or use the **Reset View** button to return to globe overview

## 🎮 **Features**

### **Smart Ground Following:**
- Camera automatically adjusts to terrain height
- Minimum altitude of 1 meter above ground
- Smooth transitions between locations

### **Visual Indicators:**
- **Red pin marker** shows your exact location
- **Location label** displays coordinates
- **WASD controls** only work when close to ground

### **Geothermal Site Integration:**
- Right-click near any geothermal site for close-up exploration
- Perfect for examining site surroundings and terrain
- Combine with site data popups for full context

## 🌟 **Use Cases**

### **Site Inspection:**
- Drop into Street View near geothermal sites
- Examine local terrain and geography
- Get a ground-level perspective of the area

### **Area Exploration:**
- Explore Nevada/California geothermal regions
- Navigate between different geological features
- Study landscape patterns and formations

### **Educational Tours:**
- Create virtual field trips to geothermal areas
- Show students ground-level perspectives
- Combine satellite and street-level views

## 🎯 **Pro Tips**

1. **Best Locations**: Try Street View near:
   - Brady Hot Springs
   - Desert Peak
   - Stillwater Geothermal Area

2. **Smooth Navigation**: 
   - Use mouse to look around first
   - Then use WASD to move in the direction you're looking

3. **Quick Exploration**:
   - Right-click multiple locations to "teleport"
   - Each right-click drops a new pin and moves you there

4. **Return to Overview**:
   - Use the green eye button for quick exit
   - Or zoom out manually with mouse wheel

## 🔧 **Technical Details**

- **Ground Height**: 10 meters above terrain
- **Movement Speed**: 50 meters per key press
- **View Range**: 360° horizontal, 180° vertical
- **Minimum Zoom**: 1 meter (super close-up possible)
